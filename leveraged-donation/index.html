The Strategy

3 Steps. One Clean Structure.

U.S. tax law allows contributors to deduct the fair market value of donated assets — not just their cost basis. ROI CFO structures a bulk mineral acquisition at a fraction of appraised FMV, then allows taxpayer-partners to donate their allocated portion at full FMV.

01

Bulk Acquisition at Discount

Taxpayer acquires a partnership interest in a Series LLC holding documented mineral assets. IRC “tacking” rules grant long-term holding period on Day 1 — no holding period required.

02

Deduction at Appraised Value

Upon donation to the qualified charity, IRS allows the deduction at full fair market value per qualified appraisal — not the lower cash outlay. This creates the 5:1 leverage ratio.

03

Documented, Compliant Exit

A completed Form 8283 (signed by appraiser + charity officer), partnership K-1, and qualified appraisal are delivered directly for use on the client’s tax return. No gaps.

The Leverage

Why 5:1 Changes the Math

Every $1 of cash invested generates a $5 charitable deduction against ordinary income. On a standard $60,000 investment for a $1M income earner, that produces a $300,000 deduction — reducing total tax by over $90,900 in real cash savings after accounting for the investment cost.

5-year carryforward.

Flexible year-end decision.

Taxpayer-partner retains the option to donate their allocated minerals to a qualified charity OR retain and explore commercial use — decision made at year end.

Related-Use Application

Applied on Tribal & Working Farmland

Donated material is applied by alliance partners on market garden plots and Tribal farmland (e.g. Oklahoma, Wind River, Lower Brule) as a soil amendment for agricultural and land-restoration use, demonstrating a real, active related-use program.

Related-Use Charitable Donation

Related-Use Charitable Donation

Donated minerals go to a qualified 501(c)(3) charity that commits in writing to use the material for exempt agricultural or land-restoration work for a minimum of three years — measurable, mission-aligned charitable impact with no circular flow of funds.

Risk & Compliance

Why This Stands Up

Specifically designed to avoid the pitfalls that plagued listed transactions like conservation easements. Four structural differences protect clients.

Not a Listed Transaction

The IRS has not identified this transaction type as a listed or reportable transaction. Unlike many easements, there is no disclosure requirement under Reg §1.6011-4.

Tangible Asset, Real Market

The donated asset (volcanic mineral) has an active commercial market and real buyers. The FMV is not theoretical — it reflects an actual traded commodity.

Full Documentation Package

Each investor receives: Qualified Appraisal, Form 8283 signed by appraiser and charity officer, Partnership K-1 with Code E reporting, and positive capital account basis.

Mission-Aligned Charity

Minerals are donated to qualified 501(c)(3) charities that commit in writing to a minimum 3-year related-use period on agricultural or land-restoration work — genuine, documented charitable impact with no circular flow of funds.

Conservation Easements

  • ✗Listed transaction — disclosure required
  • ✗Hypothetical value (no-development restriction)
  • ✗Active IRS audit campaigns; penalty exposure
  • ✗Charity receives non-commercializable restriction

This Strategy

  • ✓Not a listed transaction — no disclosure required
  • ✓Tangible mineral with real commercial market value
  • ✓Compliant IRC tacking + qualified appraisal structure
  • ✓Charity receives a usable, beneficial asset
