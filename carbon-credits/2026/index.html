What it is

One thing is purchased. The credit.

About $2 billion of credits are available for the 2026 tax season, so each purchase is sized to the client's liability. The IRS and the Department of Energy confirmed the project met the approval conditions.

01

The credit

A federal income tax credit created for carbon capture and storage. Under Section 6418 the holder can sell it for cash to another taxpayer.

02

The buyer

Applies the credit dollar for dollar against federal income tax on Form 3800. A $10M tax bill is paid with about $8.5M. Net savings are $1.5M.

03

The delivery

Each credit arrives with serial numbers, a Certificate of Title and Transfer and an IRS registration number. EstateSpace manages the process end to end.

Process

Six steps from first form to filed credit.

Step 01

Registration

Client registration form and ID verification. That is the only paperwork to start.

Step 02

Agreements

Placement Agreement, W-9 and Master Purchase Agreement signed. The client's CPA reviews first.

Step 03

Escrow

Wire instructions arrive by email and text. Funds go to escrow, not to the seller.

Step 04

Delivery

The seller delivers the credit and closing package directly to the client. Vintage credits deliver within 20 business days of full payment.

Step 05

Filing

The CPA claims the credit on Form 3800 with the registration number and the delivery package.

Step 06

Forward credits

EstateSpace works with the client on forward credits for the current year so next year is covered too.

Stacking

Minerals first. Then the credit.

Minerals shrink taxable income first and reach the state return. The 45Q credit then eliminates the federal tax that remains. The client buys fewer credits to reach zero.

Protections

Built into the Master Purchase Agreement.

01

Escrow

Payment is held in escrow. Vintage credits are paid into escrow at signing. Forward credits release in two stages.

02

Seller indemnity

The seller indemnifies for recapture and compliance failures tied to seller matters, including penalties and interest.

03

Replace or refund

Invalidated credits are replaced with equal type and vintage, or the price is refunded.

04

Triple reserve

For each forward credit sold, the seller generates three.

05

Audit and records

Annual third party audit right, IRS cooperation and seven years of record retention.

Adviser FAQ

The questions advisers ask first.

Q

Does the passive activity limit apply?

Counsel's opinion is that when the buyer has no ownership of or participation in the project, Section 469 does not apply by reference to that activity. The credit is a general business credit under Section 38.

Q

Does it reduce state tax?

No. 45Q is federal only. That is why it stacks with the minerals strategy.

Q

What if the client cannot use it all this year?

Unused credit carries forward 20 years and back 1 year under the general business credit rules.

Q

How much work is it for the client?

A form, an ID, three signatures and a wire.

Q

Can the CPA review everything first?

Yes. The diligence file goes to the client's CPA before the client commits.
